    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: October 30, 2026

    This policy explains what information Texpert Outdoors ("we", "us") collects when you use texpertoutdoors.com and how we use it.

    Information We Collect

    • Information you send us: your name, email, phone number and address when you place an order, contact us or sign up for offers.
    • Information stored in your browser: your cart and wishlist are saved in your own browser (local storage) so they are there when you come back. This data stays on your device.

    How We Use Your Information

    • To confirm, fulfil and ship your orders and answer your questions.
    • To send offers you have signed up for. You can unsubscribe at any time.
    • To improve our products and service.

    Sharing

    We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the store, such as shipping carriers and payment processors, and when required by law.

    Third-Party Services

    Our site loads fonts, icons and maps from third parties (Google Fonts, Cloudflare and Google Maps), which may receive your IP address when you visit.

    Your Choices

    You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing sales@texpertoutdoors.com. You can clear your saved cart and wishlist at any time by clearing your browser data.

    Contact

    Questions about this policy? Contact us at sales@texpertoutdoors.com or 2990 Congressman Ln, Dallas, TX 75220.
