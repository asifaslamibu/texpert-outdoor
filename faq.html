    Frequently Asked Questions

    Orders

    How do I place an order?

    Add items to your cart and go to checkout. Pay securely by card, or choose Pay Later and our team will call you to confirm stock and payment. You will get an order number right away.

    Which payment methods do you accept?

    Pay online by credit or debit card through Stripe's secure checkout, or choose Pay Later and pay by phone or in store when you pick up. We never see or store your card details.

    Can I change or cancel my order?

    Yes. Call us at +1-254-358-0017 or email sales@texpertoutdoors.com as soon as possible. Orders that have not shipped can usually be changed or cancelled.

    Shipping & Pickup

    How much is shipping?

    Standard shipping is free on orders of $75 or more. Orders under $75 ship for a flat $9.95.

    Where is my order?

    Use Track an Order with your order number and email, or sign in to your account to see all your orders.

    How long will my order take?

    Most orders ship within 1–2 business days and arrive within 3–7 business days. See Shipping Information for details.

    Can I pick up my order in store?

    Yes, store pickup is free at 2990 Congressman Ln, Dallas, TX 75220. Choose "Pick Up in Store" at checkout and we will contact you when your order is ready.

    Returns

    What is your return policy?

    Unused items in original packaging can be returned within 60 days for a full refund. See Returns & Exchanges.

    Are any items not returnable?

    Used scent and attractant products, opened consumables and items damaged through use cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons.

    Products

    Are your products genuine?

    Yes. Everything we sell is brand new, genuine product from the manufacturer and is covered by the manufacturer's warranty.

    Can you help me choose the right gear?

    Absolutely. Call or message us with what you are hunting or where you are headed and we will recommend options.

    Need a Hand?

    Our team is here Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm EST.
